About Me

I am Gloria Clara Nekesa, a Public Health and SRHR/HIV professional with over five years of experience supporting adolescents, young women, and communities affected by HIV/AIDS in Uganda.

My work is grounded in youth empowerment, stigma reduction, mentorship, and strengthening community-led health systems to improve access, dignity, and long-term resilience.

Leadership, Training & Community Impact

I am the Founder and CEO of Bold Minds Enterprise Limited, a youth- and women-led social enterprise based in Mbale, Uganda, empowering adolescent girls, young women (AGYW), and youth living with and affected by HIV/AIDS.

Through Bold Minds Enterprise, I lead integrated solutions including SRHR education, HIV prevention services, affordable hygiene products, and youth- and women-centered economic empowerment programs.

Community Training & Facilitation

Yes. I have facilitated trainings for community-based and constituency-led groups in both urban and rural settings.

I have worked with adolescent girls and young women, youth living with HIV, peer educators, women’s groups, youth-led community organizations, and leaders. Trainings focus on SRHR and HIV education, leadership, psychosocial support, stigma reduction, life skills, and economic empowerment.

Professional Experience & Advocacy

Began at TASO Mbale as a Young Person & Adolescent Peer Supporter (YAPS), supporting adherence, retention, and viral suppression. Later served as a Youth Peer Leader and Case Manager mentoring young people and strengthening linkages to care.

Youth HIV Advocate working with TASO Mbale, MOH, and the Mbale Network of Young People Living with HIV/AIDS (MNYPA). Co-Founder of MNYPA, advocating, mentoring, and amplifying youth voices in HIV programming.
